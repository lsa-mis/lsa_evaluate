# LSA Evaluate — live demo script (~7 minutes)

Use this click path when you reach **Slide 12 (Live demo)**. Leave the slide up or switch to the browser full screen.

**Environment:** staging or a local/dev instance with sample Hopwood-like data already prepared.

**Accounts to have ready (browser profiles or quick role switch):**

1. Applicant (student with a complete profile and at least one entry — or ability to submit)
2. Collection Administrator / Manager for a demo container
3. Judge assigned to an active judging round

If login or data fails, spend **no more than 30 seconds** troubleshooting, then use the **Fallback** under that step.

---

## Minute-by-minute

| Clock | Step | What to show |
|------:|------|----------------|
| 0:00–0:30 | Orient | “We’ll walk submission → manage → judge → award.” |
| 0:30–1:45 | Applicant | Login, profile cue, open contest, entry |
| 1:45–3:00 | Container | Program home and what’s inside it |
| 3:00–4:15 | Instance | This year’s contest settings (high level) |
| 4:15–5:45 | Judging | Rank / comment in a round |
| 5:45–7:00 | Awards | Attach prize + mention exports |

---

## Step 1 — Login and home (0:30)

1. Open the app home page.
2. Sign in with U-M SAML (or your prepared demo login).
3. Land on the signed-in home / interaction chooser.

**Say:** “Everyone starts here with University login. What you see next depends on your role.”

**Fallback:** Stay on the public welcome screen and narrate roles from the slide.

---

## Step 2 — Applicant path (~1 minute)

1. As the applicant user, open the applicant dashboard / available contests.
2. Point at an **open** contest instance the student is eligible for.
3. Either:
   - Open an **existing entry** (title, category, file, answers), or
   - Start a new submission and stop after showing title + file upload + a question field (do not complete a full submit unless you have time).
4. Point out any **status** cues on the entry list.

**Say:** “Profile once, then submit work into this year’s open window. Status cues reduce ‘did you get my email?’”

**Routes (conceptual):** home → applicant dashboard → contest instance → new/show entry.

**Fallback:** Screenshot of an entry detail, or describe: title, uploaded manuscript, application answers, eligibility checks.

---

## Step 3 — Container (program home) (~1 minute)

1. Switch to the Collection Administrator account (or same user if they have that role).
2. Open **Containers** and select the demo container (e.g. Hopwood-like program).
3. On the container show page, point out:
   - Contest descriptions list
   - Staff assignments
   - That categories, awards, and application questions live at this level

**Say:** “This is the filing cabinet — the program’s home. Staff own this space.”

**Fallback:** Narrate hierarchy from Slide 6 without clicking.

---

## Step 4 — Contest instance (~1 minute)

1. Open a contest description → an **active** (or recent) contest instance.
2. Skim only the highlights (do not edit):
   - Open / close dates
   - Categories and class-level eligibility
   - Max entries per applicant
   - Access mode (public capability URL vs invite list) if visible
3. If time: mention **setup** areas (questions, review process) without deep-diving.

**Say:** “The instance is this year’s folder — dates, rules, and who can apply.”

**Fallback:** Read those bullets from the staff slide; skip the screen.

---

## Step 5 — Judging round (~1.5 minutes)

1. Switch to the **Judge** account (or open Judge dashboard).
2. Open the assigned contest / active judging round.
3. Open **Evaluate** (or entry rankings) for the round.
4. Show:
   - List of entries to review
   - Rank field
   - Internal / external comments
   - Finalize (mention — click only if safe on staging)
5. Optionally, as staff, show that **select for next round** / complete round is a staff action after judges finish.

**Say:** “Judges rank and comment in rounds. Advancing to the next round is a staff decision.”

**Fallback:** Judge dashboard list only, or describe multi-round screening → final panel.

---

## Step 6 — Awards and exports (~1 minute)

1. As staff, open the contest instance **awards panel** (or container winners).
2. Point at an entry marked winner/finalist or attach a sample **primary** / **add-on** award if safe.
3. Mention without clicking every export:
   - Export entries / round results
   - Awards roster
   - Disbursement export
4. Mention award notice email as the “notify” step.

**Say:** “Decisions become recorded awards and files finance and the program can use — no side spreadsheet required for the source of truth.”

**Fallback:** Container reports page or winners view; list export names from Slide 11.

---

## Close the demo

1. Return to the slide deck on **Slide 13 — What this unlocks for LSA**.
2. One sentence: “Same story you just saw: one platform from open call through award.”
